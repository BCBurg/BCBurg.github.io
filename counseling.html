Going Deeper

Sometimes the Journey
Needs More Than a Weekend

Professional Christian counseling available through Mend Counseling in Vicksburg, Mississippi.

We built the intensive for the weekend.
We built this page for the rest of the journey.

Every couple that walks into a REFLECT/REFRESH intensive brings a history. Some of that history surfaces over two days and finds resolution. Some of it surfaces and reminds you that there is more work to do — work that benefits from a trained guide walking alongside you week after week.

That is not failure. That is wisdom. Recognizing when you need support is one of the most courageous things a married couple can do.

"You may not see in yourself what your spouse sees. And you may not be able to fix what ails you alone. Licensed Christian counselors exist for exactly that moment. Don't be afraid to reach out."
Rule 7 — REFLECT/REFRESH Post-Journey Check-In

Candi Crawford is that counselor. Through Mend Counseling in Vicksburg, Mississippi, she offers licensed professional marriage counseling to couples who are ready to go further than the intensive could take them.

Candi Crawford, LPC, NCC
Owner, Mend Counseling — Vicksburg, MS
PhD Candidate, Colorado Christian University

Candi is a Licensed Professional Counselor and National Certified Counselor with over fifteen years of experience working with individuals and couples. Her clinical specialties include marriage and relational counseling, trauma, communication, and emotional safety. She is EMDR trained and operates from a person-centered, trauma-informed foundation rooted in Christian faith.

As co-creator of REFLECT/REFRESH, Candi brings the same warmth, clinical depth, and pastoral sensitivity to her counseling practice that she brings to every intensive. She creates environments where couples feel seen, heard, and safe enough to do the real work.
